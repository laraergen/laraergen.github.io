About Me

Business-minded. Research-driven.

I am an undergraduate student at Rutgers Business School studying Supply Chain Management and pursuing a concentration in Professional Selling. My interests center on how businesses source, operate, and grow—particularly in consumer packaged goods, beauty, fashion, and luxury retail.

Through internship and academic projects, I have explored public procurement, historical bid data, market research, competitor positioning, and go-to-market strategy. I enjoy translating complex information into organized insights that can support practical business decisions.

I’m especially interested in the connection between supply chain operations and the customer-facing side of business: how sourcing, pricing, marketing, and execution work together to create value.
